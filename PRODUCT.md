# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: framework-free HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for a lightweight GitHub Pages site.

## Users

People discovering Digital Futures Initiative and prospective volunteers, mentors, or partners looking for an official way to connect.

## Product Purpose

A publishable public introduction that explains the initiative at a high level and routes visitors to its verified social channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Responsive static hosting on GitHub Pages; Formspree contact submission once a verified destination email exists. No unverified statistics, programme records, events, contact details, testimonials, or other claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Verified Facebook and TikTok URLs supplied by the user. Local AI-generated images are illustrative only and are labelled as such.
