What guides us

Digital opportunity grows when people can learn, create, and connect.

Digital Futures Initiative is a space for curiosity, practical learning, and a more confident digital future.

Our focus

Learn. Create.
Innovate. Connect.

01

Learn

Build core digital literacy — from using everyday tools with confidence to a first introduction to code.

02

Create

Turn ideas into real projects — apps, content, and designs learners can point to and say, "I made that."

03

Innovate

Apply technology to problems that matter close to home — in schools, families, and communities.

04

Connect

Bring learners together with mentors, schools, and industry, so no one has to figure it out alone.

About DFI

Still early, and building in the open.

Digital Futures Initiative is a programme of Lapologa Foundation, working to help young people across Botswana build practical digital skills, confidence, and opportunity.

We're at an early stage — much of what's on this page describes intent rather than a finished track record. As programmes actually launch, we'll update this page to show what's really happening, not before.

A shared horizon

The next chapter starts with access, attention, and imagination.

We believe the digital future is something people should be able to participate in — together.
